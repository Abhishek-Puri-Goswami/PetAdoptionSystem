# Pet Care Basics

General guidance for new pet owners, organized by the energy level and
temperament labels used on pet profiles in this platform.

## Energy level

- **LOW energy pets** are content with short walks (10-15 minutes) and
  indoor play. They suit apartments and owners with limited free time.
- **MEDIUM energy pets** need roughly 30-45 minutes of active exercise
  per day, split across one or two walks or play sessions.
- **HIGH energy pets** need 60+ minutes of vigorous exercise daily and
  do best with a yard or frequent outdoor access. Without enough
  exercise, high-energy pets are more likely to develop destructive
  habits indoors.

## Temperament

- **CALM** pets adapt well to quiet households and are generally good
  with first-time owners.
- **PLAYFUL** pets need regular interactive play (toys, fetch) to stay
  content and are often a good match for families with children.
- **INDEPENDENT** pets are comfortable spending time alone and don't
  need constant attention, but still need daily check-ins.
- **AFFECTIONATE** pets bond closely with their owners and may
  experience separation anxiety if left alone for long stretches.
- **PROTECTIVE** pets are alert and watchful; early socialization with
  new people and animals is especially important for this temperament.

## First two weeks after adoption

- Keep the environment calm and predictable; avoid large gatherings or
  major changes in the first few days.
- Establish a consistent feeding and walking schedule immediately.
- Schedule a check-up with a local veterinarian within the first two
  weeks, even if the pet had a health check before adoption.
- Watch for signs of stress (hiding, loss of appetite, excessive
  vocalizing) — these are normal initially but should improve within
  one to two weeks.

This guidance is general. Always follow specific instructions from the
adopting shelter or a veterinarian for an individual pet's needs.
