# Adoption Fees

Adoption fees vary by shelter and are set independently by each shelter,
not by the platform. As a general guide, most shelters on this platform
charge in these typical ranges:

- **Dogs:** $50–$300, depending on age, breed, and whether the dog is
  already vaccinated and neutered/spayed.
- **Cats:** $30–$150, with similar factors affecting the price.
- **Puppies and kittens** (under 6 months) are usually priced at the
  higher end of these ranges, since they typically need more upcoming
  veterinary care.
- **Senior pets** (7+ years) are frequently offered at a reduced fee or
  waived fee to encourage adoption, depending on the shelter's current
  policy.

What the fee typically covers:
- An initial veterinary health check.
- Core vaccinations already administered before listing.
- Spay/neuter surgery, if already completed prior to listing.

What the fee does not cover:
- Ongoing veterinary care after adoption.
- Pet supplies (crate, food, leash, etc.).
- Transportation to pick up the pet.

Exact fees for a specific pet are listed on that pet's profile and should
always be confirmed directly with the shelter before applying, since
shelter-specific pricing can change.
