# Adoption Process

Adopting a pet through this platform follows five steps:

1. **Browse pets.** Any registered adopter can browse the list of available
   pets and view each pet's profile, including species, breed, age,
   temperament, and energy level.
2. **Submit an application.** An adopter submits one adoption application
   per pet, including a short note about why they'd be a good fit. An
   adopter cannot have two active applications for the same pet at once.
3. **Shelter review.** Shelter staff and shelter admins review pending
   applications. A shelter admin may schedule an in-person appointment to
   meet the pet before deciding.
4. **Approval or rejection.** A shelter admin approves or rejects the
   application. Approving one application for a pet automatically rejects
   any other pending applications for that same pet, since a pet can only
   be adopted once.
5. **Pickup.** Once approved, the adopter coordinates pickup directly with
   the shelter. The platform does not currently handle payment or delivery
   logistics for pickup.

Typical review time is a few business days, though it varies by shelter
and how many applications they are handling.

An adopter can view the status of their own applications at any time
(pending, approved, or rejected) but cannot see other adopters'
applications.
